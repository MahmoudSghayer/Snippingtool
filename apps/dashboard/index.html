How it keeps you safer

Limits first. Profit second.

Every action Nova Trade takes goes through a safety governor before it happens. When a limit is reached, the action doesn't happen.

A safety governor with hard caps

It caps actions per hour, how long a session runs, how often you buy compared with how often you search, and how many coins move per hour, with cooldowns between actions. You can tighten the limits; you can't loosen them past your plan's ceiling.

  • 30 actions / h
  • 90 min sessions
  • buy : search ≤ 0.35
  • 20 s cooldown

A live risk-budget meter

See how much of each limit the current session has used while it runs, so you can stop on your own terms before the governor stops you.

A remote kill switch

One switch halts every running session at once, not just the tab in front of you.

It uses the game's own web app

Nova Trade works through EA's own web app in your browser, the same one you use by hand. It never forges or replays requests to EA's servers.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is Nova Trade undetectable?

No. No tool is, and anyone who promises “undetectable” is guessing. Anything that automates the web app leaves a pattern, and EA decides what it acts on.

What we can do is keep your activity inside limits: fewer actions per hour, shorter sessions, more searching than buying, cooldowns, and a hard stop when a limit is hit. That lowers the risk. It doesn't make it zero, and we won't say it does.

What happens if I get banned?

EA decides bans and restrictions, and we can't reverse them. A ban or restriction is not a reason for a refund, including within the first 24 hours. See section 2 of the Terms of Service.

Can I get a refund?

Only if you ask within 24 hours of your purchase. After that, no refunds are given. The full rules, and how to ask, are in the Refund Policy.

Does it work on console?

Only through the EA FC web app, in Chrome on a computer. Nothing runs on the console itself.
